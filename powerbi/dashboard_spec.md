# Dashboard Specification — four pages

The Power BI report (and the HTML preview generated by `build_dashboard.py`)
follows this layout. Each page names the visuals, the measures behind them, and
the business question it answers. All measures are defined in `dax_measures.md`.

## Page 1 — Executive summary

*Question: are we on plan, and where is the money going?*

- **KPI cards** (top row): `Total Actual`, `Total Budget`, `Variance $` with
  `Variance %` delta (red up / green down), `Headcount FTE`, count of material
  variances. Latest-month context via the date slicer.
- **Forecast vs actual** (line): actual `Headcount FTE` history + the champion's
  6-month forecast with an 80% prediction-interval band. Names the model.
- **Top variances** (diverging bar): largest line-item variances, red = over
  budget (unfavourable), blue = under. Sorted by absolute dollars.

## Page 2 — Variance drill-through

*Question: why did spend break plan?*

- **Budget vs actual by department** (grouped column): `Total Budget` vs
  `Total Actual` per department, latest month. Drill-through: department →
  cost centre → account category.
- **Salary driver attribution** (diverging bar): volume + mix + rate, which
  reconcile to total salary variance. The interview centrepiece — it explains
  *why*, not just *how much*.
- **Total spend over time** (line): `Total Budget` vs `Total Actual` by month;
  the post-reorg divergence is visible.

## Page 3 — Workforce analytics

*Question: how is the workforce composed and staffed against plan?*

- **Headcount composition** (stacked column): `Employee FTE` + `Contractor FTE`
  by month, with a marker on the structural-break month.
- **Vacancy rate by department** (diverging bar): `Vacancy Rate` = (planned −
  filled) ÷ planned; negative = staffed above plan.
- **Contractor share by department** (bar): `Contractor Share`.

## Page 4 — Model health

*Question: can we trust the forecast, and is the data clean?*

- **Forecast accuracy by target** (bar): champion `Forecast MAPE`, colour-graded
  good/ok/warning. From the rolling-origin backtest.
- **Data quality** (bar): quarantined records by reason — nothing silently
  dropped.
- **Drift & retraining** (table): per target, drift status, what triggered
  retraining, and the challenger's improvement over the champion.

## Design rules

- **One date table** (`dim_date`) drives all time intelligence.
- Headcount always via the **semi-additive** measures — never an implicit sum.
- Diverging colour (blue/red) only for signed variance; status colours reserved
  for model-health state and always paired with a label.
- Every figure traces to a warehouse table or a `data/reports/` artifact.
