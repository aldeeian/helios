What am I looking at?

Helios forecasts headcount and spend for a simulated ~700-person company, compares actuals against the plan, and explains the gaps. Pick a month and a department above; everything below updates.

  • Budget — what the plan said this month would cost.
  • Actual — what it really cost.
  • Variance — actual minus budget. Positive = over budget.
  • FTE — full-time equivalents. One person at 50% time = 0.5 FTE.
  • Vacancy — planned headcount not yet filled. When actual staffing exceeds the plan this flips to Over plan.
  • Material — the variance is big enough to act on (≥5% and ≥$50k).
  • Anomaly — unusual for this line's own history, not just large.

All data is synthetic, generated from a seed. A deliberate reorganisation at month 24 breaks the forecast — that is the event the drift monitors are meant to catch.
